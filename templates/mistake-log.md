# Mistake Log: <subject>

One per subject, in the repo next to its study cycle. Feeds Anki drafting and weekly
review. Every confirmed miss from quizzing, practice questions, or mocks gets a row —
including the embarrassing ones; those are the highest-value rows.

| Date | What I got wrong | Kind | Root cause | Fixed by | Card? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YYYY-MM-DD | <the actual wrong claim/move> | slip / gap / misconception | <why — the belief behind it> | <re-derivation, practice, note> | queued/no/-- |

Rules:
- **Kind matters.** Slips need no card. Gaps need teaching. Misconceptions need
  dislodging — probe their extent before marking fixed.
- A row is closed only after a later retrieval succeeds (quiz, practice, or Anki).
- Recurring root causes get surfaced in the weekly review.
