## Facts you may use (source ids)
- Herders must move animals to fresh grass all year, so they never stay in one place. [S1]
- Four seasonal pastures: Qystau = winter, shelter, hay is fed, weak animals stay; Kokteu = spring, near the winter land, fresh grass;
  Zhailau = summer, climb to high rich grass, animals fatten, milk and koumiss; Kuzeu = autumn, step back south before the cold, return to Qystau by water. [S1]
- Following water means grass is never eaten down to bare ground. [S1]
- Example: a Saryarka family winters on the Syrdarya and summers about 1000 km north near Atbasar. [S1]
- Because all clans move on the same tracks, they must agree who grazes where. Judges called Biys decide disputes by Adat (old custom law). [S2]
- Vocabulary: aul = a camp of related families; Adat = old custom law; Biy = judge.
