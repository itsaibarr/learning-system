# Exam profile: Pearson Edexcel International A Level (IAL)

Checked against the real documents on 2026-10-07/08 (`docs/research/teaching-quality/exam-mode.md`).

## Codes and citations

- Paper reference `WMA11/01` = Pure Mathematics 1, paper 01. Series: January, June ("Summer"), October.
- Cite: **`WMA11/01 June 2024 Q2(ii)`** (add the log number, e.g. P75707A, if two papers could be confused).
- Official file names: `wma11-01-que-YYYYMMDD.pdf` (exam date), `-rms-` mark scheme and `-pef-` examiners'
  report (results date).

## Paper

WMA11: one paper, 1 h 30, 75 marks, all questions compulsory, calculator and formula booklet allowed.
"Inexact answers should be given to three significant figures unless otherwise stated." Some questions say
"In this question you must show all stages of your working. Solutions relying on calculator technology are
not acceptable." Those questions give no marks to unworked answers.

## Command words

The IAL maths specification has no command-word list. The UK A level poster (unverified for IAL) defines
**Show that** "All steps need to be shown to get the given answer, followed by a conclusion"; **Hence** "Use
the result or information from a previous part of the question"; **State / Write down** "Provide an answer
concisely, without needing to show the working". Treat the instructions printed in each paper as binding.

## Mark schemes (June 2024 MS p.4)

**M** method marks ("knowing a method and attempting to apply it"); **A** accuracy marks, only with the
relevant M mark; **B** unconditional accuracy marks; **dM1** dependent method mark. Abbreviations: `ft`
follow through, `cao` correct answer only, `cso` correct solution only ("There must be no errors in this
part"), `awrt` answers which round to, `isw` ignore subsequent working, `oe` or equivalent, `SC` special
case, `ag` answer given. Rubric points for typed questions copy these codes: `M1: differentiates, a power
decreased by 1`, `A1: gradient = 7`.

## Examiners' reports

Principal Examiner Feedback: general comments, then per question. Example (WMA11/01 June 2024 Q2(ii)):
"A significant number of candidates did not show the working for rationalising the denominator and hence
lost the final two marks."
